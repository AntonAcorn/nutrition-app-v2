Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Rumbly Eats ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal nutrition tracking application. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and display name when you register.
  • Nutrition data: meals you log, calorie and macro values, food descriptions you type or dictate.
  • Body data: weight entries you record, height, age, and fitness goal you set in your profile.
  • Photos of food: images you upload for AI analysis. Photos are used solely to identify food items and estimate nutrition — they are not shared or used for any other purpose.
  • Usage analytics: anonymised product events (e.g. "photo analysed", "meal logged") sent to PostHog to help us understand how the app is used. No personally identifiable information is included in these events.
  • Push notification tokens: if you enable reminders, we store your browser push subscription and timezone to send daily logging reminders.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with anyone except the sub-processors listed below.

Sub-processors

  • OpenAI — food photos and meal descriptions are sent to OpenAI's API for nutritional analysis. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to this data.
  • PostHog — anonymised usage analytics (EU region servers).

Data storage

All data is stored on servers in the EU. Your nutrition history, body data, and account information are kept for as long as your account exists.

Your rights

  • Delete your account: go to the Me tab → Delete account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers immediately.
  • Export or access your data: contact us at privacy@rumblyeats.org and we will provide a copy within 30 days.
  • Correct your data: most data can be edited directly in the app. For anything else, contact us.

Data retention

When you delete your account, all personal data is deleted immediately and permanently. Anonymised analytics events are retained by PostHog per their own policy.

Children

Rumbly Eats is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@rumblyeats.org
