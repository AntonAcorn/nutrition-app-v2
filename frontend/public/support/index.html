Common questions

The photo analyzer got my meal wrong. Why?

AI photo recognition is a starting point, not a final answer. Always glance at the calorie and macro numbers before saving — tap any field to fix it. Over time, saved meals will be one-tap re-logs that don't need AI guessing.

How does the calorie bank work?

Every day you stay under your calorie target deposits up to 200 kcal into your bank. Days over target can withdraw up to 200 kcal — your streak holds. You also get two "relax days" per month where overage doesn't withdraw at all (toggle a relax day from the bank badge on your dashboard).

My streak says it broke even though I stayed under target.

The logging streak counts days you logged at least one meal — not days you stayed under target. If you didn't log anything yesterday, the streak pauses. Logging anything (even one snack) keeps it warm.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: go to the Me tab → Delete account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers immediately. If you can't access the app for any reason, email support@rumblyeats.org and we'll handle it for you.

Apple Health isn't syncing my steps.

Open the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Health → Rumbly Eats and confirm permissions are granted. Steps and active calories will appear on the dashboard within a minute.

I'm getting too many push notifications.

Coach pushes fire only when something is worth flagging — never more than once a day. If you want fewer, go to the Me tab and turn off coach notifications. Daily logging reminders are configured separately in the same tab.

Bugs and crashes

If something crashes or behaves unexpectedly, email support@rumblyeats.org with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Crash reports are also sent automatically (anonymously) so we can spot issues quickly.
